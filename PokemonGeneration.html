The Reigons of Pokemon

Pokemon as of today November of 2026 has 9 released reigons 3 of which introduce reigonal vairients. Each based off of a unique country or reigon within for example: Kalos based on France. The reigons in generational order go; Kanto(based on kanto reigon Japan), Johto(based on Kansai reigon in Japan), Hoenn(kyushu island Japan), Sinnoh(based on Hokkaido Japan), Unova(Nyc and New York) Kalos(Based on France) Alola(basedon Hawaii), Galar(based on the UK) Paldea(Iberian Peninsula). Each of the focus both on making it more relatable to people around the world and introducing unique gimmicks such as Mega evolution, Z moves and Gigantimax. All the reigons feature not only simlar landmarks but pokemon that represent things of importance of the reigon like Galar has a soccer pokemon and a punk music pokemon.
